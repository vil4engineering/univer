# Groundhog Day Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

## Overview

Groundhog Day is a personal diary application. It is designed to keep diary data on your device and does not currently operate its own backend or user account system.

Some features use Apple platform capabilities and may interact with third-party services when you explicitly choose to share content or connect a service.

## Data stored on your device

Groundhog Day stores a small amount of diary data locally on your device so the app can function:

- the diary start date
- your current working draft
- the selected daily line state used to keep the same line for the same diary day

This information is stored using Apple platform local persistence mechanisms. Groundhog Day does not currently sync this data to a Groundhog Day server.

## Sharing

Groundhog Day can generate Day Card images locally on your device. Your draft text is user-authored content.

Sharing happens only after an explicit user action. When you choose to share, selected content may be passed to the iOS system sharing interface and then to a third-party app or service you select.

After you share, the receiving third-party app or service processes the shared content under its own privacy policy and terms. Groundhog Day does not control how third parties store, process, or distribute content once you send it to them.

## LinkedIn integration

Groundhog Day may offer an optional connection to LinkedIn for user-authorized publishing.

If this functionality is enabled in the future, Groundhog Day will request only the LinkedIn permissions required for the publishing action presented to you. Groundhog Day will not publish to LinkedIn without an explicit user action.

Content selected for publication may include your diary text and a locally generated Day Card image. LinkedIn processes LinkedIn account data and published content according to LinkedIn's own privacy policy.

## Analytics and tracking

Groundhog Day currently does not use third-party analytics SDKs, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not perform cross-app tracking.

## Data retention and deletion

Locally stored diary data remains on your device until it is changed or removed by you, or until the app’s data is removed through normal iOS mechanisms (for example, deleting the app).

Content you share to a third-party app or service is subject to that third party’s retention and deletion controls.

If direct LinkedIn integration is introduced, authorization can be revoked through applicable LinkedIn account controls.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s functionality changes. The “Last updated” date above will be revised to reflect changes.
