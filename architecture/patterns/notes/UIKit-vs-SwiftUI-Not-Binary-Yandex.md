## Digest

Production apps mix ObjC legacy, UIKit, SwiftUI, experiments, analytics, **server-driven UI** (e.g. DivKit). Question is safest maintainable choice per feature — not “SwiftUI everywhere.” Bridging cost (hosting, navigation, shared state) is the real tax. Offline-heavy apps still lean native.
