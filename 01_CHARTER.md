## Mission

Engineering Builder — **long-term engineering education system** (на ~10 лет) для:

1. **Student A** — рост от experienced iOS engineer к broadly capable **Software Engineer** с глубоким пониманием modern software systems **including AI**.  
2. **Student B** — интерес и вход в engineering world через ту же карту, более лёгкие Levels.

Это не knowledge dump. Это **living university**.

Публичность репозитория допустима; mass public audience — **не** product goal.

---

## Long-term goal

Стать Software Engineer, способным **design, build, deploy и evolve** software systems across domains.

- **Mobile engineering** — сильнейший commercial background (foundation, не потолок).  
- **AI** — одновременно:
  - **engineering tool** (AI-assisted Software Engineering);
  - **engineering discipline** (AI Technology: как системы устроены).

Цель — **не** «стать AI Engineer».  
Цель — Software Engineer, который **deeply understands** modern systems, including AI.

---

## Distinguishing principle

**Учим не «что думать».**  
**Учим «как принимать engineering decisions».**

| Typical format | Optimizes for | Usually skips |
|----------------|---------------|---------------|
| Knowledge base | Finding notes | Practice, proof, reuse |
| Video course | Consuming lessons | Ownership, constraints |
| Framework book | Syntax / APIs | Organizational trade-offs |
| Interview cheat sheet | Memorized answers | Natural competence |
| **Engineering Builder** | **Decision quality under uncertainty** | Trivia и folder tourism |

---

## Educational pyramid (Completeness)

Knowledge считается complete только когда проходит вверх:

```text
Teach
  ▲
Reuse
  ▲
Practice
  ▲
Demonstrate
  ▲
Explain
  ▲
Understand
```

Reading / watching / collecting notes **не** равны learning. Нужен application.

## Teaching levels

| Level | Audience | Depth |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **1** | Student B | Intuition, analogies, visuals |
| **2** | Engineer | Professional explanation, examples |
| **3** | Deep Dive | Implementation, trade-offs, history, research |

Каждый major topic стремится иметь Levels 1–3. Default study часто = Level 2.

## Repository principles

Single Source of Truth · Living documentation · Executable examples · Reusable knowledge · Cross-links · Architecture over folders · Automation over manual copy · Docsify UI через текущий ship.

## Guiding principles (binding)

Software engineering over framework knowledge · Systems thinking over memorization · First principles over recipes · Practice over passive reading · Architecture over implementation trivia · Understanding over copying · Curiosity over trends · Evidence over hype · AI as a tool · AI as a technology · Continuous experimentation · Teach to learn.

## Anti-goals

- Wikipedia sprawl  
- Foo/Bar demos без problem  
- «Стать AI Engineer» как identity  
- Поглощение private `career` в public repo
