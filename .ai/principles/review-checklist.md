# Principle: Review Checklist (meta)

Shared quality bar used by Reviewer and by Authors before handoff.

This is not a role. For the Reviewer operating mode see [../roles/reviewer.md](../roles/reviewer.md).

## Always look for

- Factual errors
- Duplicated information (same explanation elsewhere)
- Weak or vague explanations
- Unsupported statements (speculation as fact)
- Missing prerequisites
- Broken or stale links
- Inconsistent terminology
- Opportunities for diagrams
- Opportunities for labs
- Opportunities for production examples / failure modes
- Violations of [../constitution.md](../constitution.md)
- Violations of SoT / graph rules

## Disposition

- **Reject** weak content that cannot be fixed lightly.
- **Request changes** when fixable.
- **Do not rewrite** unless necessary; prefer concrete findings.
- Architecture/structure issues → escalate to Architect role.
